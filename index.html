Line 10

A transit-wayfinding skin for Video.js v10. Use the switches to test worst-case footage, a phone-sized player and other line colours, then put the mouse away and drive it from the keyboard.

Keyboard test

  1. Press Tab until the player has a focus ring, then Space to play.
  2. Press 0–9 to jump between the ten stops on the line; ← → seek, ↑ ↓ change volume, M mutes, F goes fullscreen.
  3. Press ? for the keyboard timetable and Esc to close it.
  4. Keep pressing Tab to reach every control; arrow keys move the sliders.
  5. Press < or > to adjust playback speed, or press and hold anywhere on the playing video for 2×.

Specs and accessibility

Built on
@videojs/html 10.3.0 UI components, composed from the Scaffold skin source
Dependencies
None beyond Video.js: no webfont, icon library or framework
Contrast
17.1:1 for text on the sign over pure white footage, the worst case
Touch targets
48 × 48 px minimum, held at any scale
Forced colors
Route, station stops and focus rings redrawn in system colours
Transparency and motion
Opaque sign for reduced transparency; instant state changes for reduced motion
Several players
IDs are scoped per instance, so players on one page never share a popup
